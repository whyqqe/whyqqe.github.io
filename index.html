About

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My research interests lie in embodied AI and robot learning, with a focus on vision-language-action models, long-horizon manipulation, and memory mechanisms that let agents keep track of task progress over extended interaction.
